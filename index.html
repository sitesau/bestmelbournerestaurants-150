Common questions

How do I choose between a high rating from few reviews and a lower rating from many reviews? A high rating from a small number of reviews suggests a potentially excellent experience for a limited sample, while a lower rating from many reviews indicates a broader, more consistent public perception. Consider which type of feedback is more relevant to your decision-making process.

What information should I prepare before contacting a restaurant? Prepare details such as the date, time, and number of guests for your reservation. If applicable, note any dietary restrictions, specific menu requirements, or if you are inquiring about private dining or functions.

Can I rely solely on the category listed for a restaurant? While categories provide a useful starting point, they should be used in conjunction with other information. Always check the venue's specific offerings, menu, and reviews to ensure it meets your expectations, especially for specialised needs like vegan or fine dining.
